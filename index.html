Now Playing

Nothing between you and the music.

Big artwork, open controls and a queue you can edit. The same controls follow you to the notification, the lock screen, your headset and Bluetooth, and stay in sync with the app.

Library

All of it. Right here.

Resona finds the music already on your phone and lays it out the way you think about it.

  • Songs
  • Albums
  • Artists
  • Folders
  • Recently Added
  • Search everything. Songs, albums and artists from one field, fast even with thousands of tracks.
  • Shape what's next. Reorder the queue, remove songs, or clear what's upcoming.
  • Keep what you love. Favorites, playlists and recently played, saved on your phone.
Artwork

Every song sets the room.

The cover of whatever's playing becomes a soft backdrop behind Home, your library and Now Playing. Change the song, change the light.

No signal. Still yours.

On a plane, underground, or somewhere without a single bar, your library is still there, because it never left your phone.

  • No account.

    Playback, collections and customization work without signing up for anything.

  • No subscription.

    Nothing to rent and nothing that expires. It plays the music on your phone.

  • No connection needed.

    Your library, playback and playlists work offline. The preview doesn't even ask for internet access.

  • Right where you left off.

    Your last song and queue come back when you do, paused and ready.

Gestures, if you want them.

  • Swipe the artwork to skip.
  • Double-tap to favorite.
  • Swipe up for the queue.

Tune the sound.

A five-band equalizer with presets, and crossfade from off to 12 seconds so one song can melt into the next. Themes too: System, Light, Dark or AMOLED.
